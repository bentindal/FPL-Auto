**Candidate advanced features (from MODEL_IMPROVEMENT.md and CONTEXT.md):**

1. **Seasonal bucket (GW_bucket)** — Season progression effect
   - Values: 'early' (GW 1-10), 'mid' (GW 11-20), 'late' (GW 21-30), 'final' (GW 31-38)
   - Expected impact: Moderate (players may perform differently by season phase)
   - Hypothesis: Model accounts for fatigue/adaptation across season

2. **Form momentum (influence_trend)** — Direction & magnitude of recent form change
   - Formula: influence_rolling_5gw - influence_rolling_10gw (recent vs. longer-term)
   - Values: positive (improving), negative (declining), near-zero (stable)
   - Expected impact: Moderate (signal of momentum vs. stagnation)
   - Hypothesis: Trending up = valuable, trending down = avoid

3. **Ownership differential (selected_yoy_change)** — Year-over-year ownership delta
   - Compare current season selected% to same player in previous season (if available)
   - Expected impact: Low-Moderate (differential picks signal model gaps)
   - Hypothesis: If top 100 managers own player more than historical average, signals undervalued

4. **Injury risk flag (recent_minutes_drop)** — Sudden reduction in recent minutes
   - Formula: if minutes_rolling_5gw < 0.5 * minutes_rolling_10gw, flag as 1; else 0
   - Expected impact: Low-Moderate (avoids injured/benched players)
   - Hypothesis: Minutes drop signals upcoming absence or loss of form

5. **Team form (team_recent_wins)** — Team's recent performance
   - Proxy: Count of wins in last 5 GW (if available from team_list data)
   - Expected impact: Low (team-level, less predictive than player-level)
   - Hypothesis: Strong team form correlates with better player performances

6. **Position scarcity (position_usage_rate)** — What % of position players are used in squad
   - E.g., GK: 2 players in squad, expected minutes = 90/2 = 45 per GK on average
   - Actual position_usage = (player_minutes / (90 / squad_count)) clipped to [0, 1]
   - Expected impact: Low-Moderate (bench players may have high potential if position has injuries)
   - Hypothesis: Players in high-scarcity positions (few bench options) more likely to play

7. **Expected points trend (xp_trend)** — Change in xP predictions across GWs
   - E.g., compare xP from GW(t-1) to xP from GW(t)
   - Expected impact: Low (available only after GW t-1 complete; risky for lookahead bias)
   - Status: SKIP if risks temporal integrity; include only if careful about data availability

8. **Interaction: strength_attack * form**
   - Formula: strength_attack_home * influence_rolling_5gw
   - Expected impact: Moderate (strong teams benefit from good form players)
   - Hypothesis: Form matters less if team weak; form matters more if team strong

9. **Interaction: fixture_difficulty * efficiency**
   - Formula: (strength_defence_home) * (efficiency_goals_per_90)
   - Expected impact: Low-Moderate (strong defenders harder to break down)
   - Hypothesis: High-efficiency FWD vs. strong DEF = lower xP

10. **Lag feature: previous_GW_points**
    - Access total_points from previous GW (GW t-1) for same player
    - Expected impact: High (best single-GW predictor of next GW)
    - Risk: May be high-VIF with other features; include if VIF < 5 after testing
    - Hypothesis: Last-GW points are strong form signal

**Implementation strategy:**

For each candidate, follow the iteration workflow:

1. Add feature to engineer_features_on_gw_data()
2. Run model.py on one season (e.g., 2021-22) with -repeat 19 -score_train_vs_test
3. Extract RMSE from logs, compare to Plan 04-02 baseline
4. Calculate RMSE delta: (baseline_v2_rmse - new_rmse) / baseline_v2_rmse * 100%
5. Threshold: Keep if > 2% improvement; drop if <= 2% or regression
6. Log decision to FEATURE_ITERATION_LOG.md
