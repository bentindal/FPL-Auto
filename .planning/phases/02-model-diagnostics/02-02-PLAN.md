## Task 4: Feature Hypothesis Generation

<read_first>
- Tasks 1-3 gap analysis
- CONTEXT.md for Phase 4 feature engineering scope (35-50 features, >2% RMSE improvement threshold)
- Existing features in model.py or fpl_auto/predictor.py
</read_first>

<action>
1. For each major gap identified, hypothesize a feature:

   **Hypothesis 1: Player Form (Missing from current model)**
   - Gap pattern: Undervalued MID/FWD with recent goals/assists
   - Feature: Rolling 3-GW average points (normalize by position)
   - Rationale: Top 100 picks hot players; model uses static season stats
   - Expected impact: +2-3% RMSE improvement (high confidence — form matters)
   - Priority: HIGH

   **Hypothesis 2: Rotation Risk / Squad Depth**
   - Gap pattern: Overvalued cheap FWD with low rotation risk
   - Feature: Club squad size by position, backup role indicator
   - Rationale: Model ignores rotation; top 100 weights starter role heavily
   - Expected impact: +1-2% RMSE (medium confidence — rotation is complex)
   - Priority: HIGH

   **Hypothesis 3: Age / Experience (Prime Years)**
   - Gap pattern: Undervalued peak-age players (26-29), overvalued very young players
   - Feature: Age bins (18-20, 21-25, 26-29, 30+) or quadratic age term
   - Rationale: Peak years may have different return patterns
   - Expected impact: +1-2% RMSE
   - Priority: MEDIUM

   **Hypothesis 4: Transfer Window Activity**
   - Gap pattern: Undervalued recent signings (pre-season), overvalued long-tenured cheap players
   - Feature: Weeks since transfer, transfer fee percentile
   - Rationale: Recent arrivals may be mis-priced; top 100 weights squad cohesion
   - Expected impact: +1-2% RMSE
   - Priority: MEDIUM

   **Hypothesis 5: Club Fixture Difficulty (Position-specific)**
   - Gap pattern: Undervalued cheap DEF from weak defensive clubs vs strong defensive teams
   - Feature: Club defensive strength (goals conceded per 90), position-specific
   - Rationale: Current model may use overall fixture difficulty, not position-aware
   - Expected impact: +1-2% RMSE
   - Priority: MEDIUM

   **Hypothesis 6: Within-Season Trend (Momentum)**
   - Gap pattern: Overvalued early-season standouts, undervalued late-bloomer FWD
   - Feature: Season progress indicator, trend direction (improving/declining)
   - Rationale: Top 100 adjusts for trends; model doesn't
   - Expected impact: +1-2% RMSE
   - Priority: MEDIUM

   **Hypothesis 7: Home/Away Bias**
   - Gap pattern: Undervalued players from teams with strong home advantage
   - Feature: Home/away performance split, venue advantage
   - Rationale: Model may not distinguish home/away xP separately
   - Expected impact: +0.5-1.5% RMSE (lower confidence — smaller effect)
   - Priority: LOW

   **Hypothesis 8: International Break Impact**
   - Gap pattern: Undervalued players from nations with few international matches, overvalued from heavily-called nations
   - Feature: Weeks until/since international break, player nation call-up rate
   - Rationale: Top 100 avoids high-call players; model doesn't
   - Expected impact: +0.5-1% RMSE
   - Priority: LOW

2. Create summary table (to go in FEATURE_ENGINEERING_PRIORITIES.md):
   ```
   | Feature Hypothesis | Gap It Addresses | Type | Priority | Confidence | Est. Impact |
   | Player Form (3-GW avg) | Undervalued form leaders | Temporal | HIGH | High | +2-3% RMSE |
   | Rotation Risk Indicator | Overvalued cheap FWD | Categorical | HIGH | Medium | +1-2% RMSE |
   ...
   ```
</action>
